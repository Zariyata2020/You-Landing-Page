HEY, YOU!

    The psychological thriller series You delves into the dark and twisted mind of Joe Goldberg, a seemingly charming and intelligent man with a disturbing obsession.
    You masterfully explores the blurred lines between love and obsession, examining how modern technology and social media can facilitate the invasive and controlling behaviors of a predator like Joe. Each season of the show uncovers the depths of his obsession, challenging viewers to question their own perceptions of love, privacy, and morality.

    In Season 1 of You,

    Joe Goldberg, a charming yet deeply disturbed bookstore manager in New York, becomes obsessed with Guinevere Beck, an aspiring writer. After a chance encounter, Joe begins stalking Beck, learning everything about her through social media and other invasive means. He carefully orchestrates situations to insert himself into her life, all while eliminating anyone he perceives as a threat to their relationship. As Joe and Beck's relationship develops, Joe's obsession turns increasingly dangerous. He resorts to manipulation, deceit, and even murder to keep Beck close and protect his version of their "perfect" love. However, as Beck starts to uncover the truth about Joe, the situation spirals out of control, leading to a dark and tragic conclusion.

    In Season 2 of You,

    Joe Goldberg moves to Los Angeles to escape his dark past, adopting the new identity of "Will Bettelheim." He soon becomes obsessed with Love Quinn, a chef with her own complicated past. As Joe tries to start fresh, his old habits resurface, leading him to stalk and manipulate those around him. His attempts at a new beginning are further complicated by the return of his vengeful ex, Candace, and the secrets that both he and Love are hiding. The season explores the twisted dynamics of their relationship, culminating in shocking revelations that challenge everything Joe thought he knew about love.

    In Season 3 of You,

    Joe Goldberg and Love Quinn are married and raising their newborn son, Henry, in the suburban town of Madre Linda. While they try to settle into a normal family life, Joe's old habits of obsession and stalking quickly resurface, this time fixating on their neighbor, Natalie. Meanwhile, Love struggles with her own dark impulses, leading to a volatile and dangerous dynamic between the couple. As they navigate the challenges of parenthood, suburban life, and their crumbling marriage, Joe and Love become embroiled in a series of deadly events, all while trying to maintain the appearance of a perfect family. The season delves into the complexities of marriage, the pressures of domestic life, and the dark secrets that threaten to tear everything apart.

    In Season 4 of You,

    Joe Goldberg tries to start fresh by assuming a new identity as "Jonathan Moore" in London, working as a professor. However, his past habits and obsessions resurface when he becomes entangled with a group of wealthy elites. Joe becomes the target of a mysterious blackmailer, known as the "Eat the Rich Killer," who seems to know his true identity. As he tries to uncover the killer's identity, Joe navigates through complex relationships, leading to a deadly game of cat and mouse. Throughout the season, Joe is forced to confront his inner demons and the consequences of his actions, making it one of the most intense and suspenseful chapters of the series.

    About "You"

    "You" is a psychological thriller series that explores the dark and complex nature of obsession, love, and the influence of modern technology. Delving into the mind of Joe Goldberg, the series challenges viewers to question the boundaries of privacy and morality in the digital age.
